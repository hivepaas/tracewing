The first release of tracewing, an HTTP(S) debugging proxy for everything a browser's own tools cannot see: mobile apps, desktop apps, servers and AI agents.

- **See every request.** HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2, WebSocket, server-sent events, gRPC and GraphQL, over HTTPS through a certificate authority made for your computer alone.
- **Big sessions stay smooth.** A million requests scroll at your display's pace, and large bodies stream through instead of piling up in memory.
- **Read what is inside.** JSON, XML, Protobuf, MessagePack and CBOR laid out; tokens, cookies and timings explained; two requests compared side by side.
- **Pause and edit.** Breakpoints hold a request or a response, and WebSocket messages too, until you send it on, changed or not.
- **Change traffic on the way.** Rules block, answer from a file, send to another host, rewrite headers and bodies, or slow the network down. For the rest, a few lines of JavaScript.
- **Made for AI work.** LLM API streams put back together, tokens and tool calls, and an MCP server so your AI tools can search and read what tracewing recorded.
- **Web3 too.** JSON-RPC calls and their calldata read by your contracts' ABIs, and a warning when an edit breaks a signed transaction.
- **Phones and emulators.** Set up from a QR code, with Android emulators and the iOS Simulator trusted in one click.
- **Works at work.** Through your company's proxy, NTLM included, with your company's own CA, client certificates and TLS keys for Wireshark.
- **No window needed.** `tracewing capture` records on a server or in CI, and writes HAR.
- **Made for every day.** 15 languages, light and dark, a text size of your choice, and layouts you can name. It runs on your computer and sends nothing anywhere.

tracewing is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). Everything in it is in this release; there is nothing to buy and nothing to sign in to.

## Downloads

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | `tracewing_0.1.0_windows_x86_64-setup.exe`, or the `.zip` to run from a folder |
| Debian, Ubuntu | `tracewing_0.1.0_amd64.deb`, `tracewing_0.1.0_arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `tracewing_0.1.0_x86_64.rpm`, `tracewing_0.1.0_aarch64.rpm` |

The Linux packages run on glibc 2.35 and newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12), and are not signed: `dnf` warns and goes on, and `zypper` needs `--no-gpg-checks`. The Windows installer is not signed either: SmartScreen asks the first time (More info › Run anyway). `SHA256SUMS` holds every file's SHA-256.

macOS builds follow; until then, [build it from source](https://github.com/hivepaas/tracewing/blob/main/docs/building.md) — it is one `make release`.

## After you install it

Open tracewing. Its proxy listens on 127.0.0.1:9090, and **Set up** walks through trusting the certificate authority it just made and sending this computer's traffic to it.

If tracewing is ever killed while it holds the system proxy, the machine loses the network until the setting goes back. tracewing puts it back by itself within a minute, and `tracewing recover` does it at once.
