A small release after 0.1.0: one scroll bar everywhere, a bar on every page that scrolls, and the Apps tab telling the truth about HTTP/3.

- **One scroll bar, everywhere.** Every bar in the window is now the same one. It is thin at rest, as wide as the grip on a divider between panels, and grows under the pointer or while you drag it. Some things that went wrong with the old bars are gone with them:
  - letting go of a body's bar over a token no longer opens what it reads;
  - dragging a body's bar no longer stops when the pointer leaves the pane;
  - on a short body, the thumb no longer drifts away from the pointer;
  - the thumb of a field of several lines now matches the field;
  - the pointer is an arrow over every bar, not an I-beam.
- **A page longer than the window shows it.** Set up, Rules, Sessions, Compose, Paused, Compare, the list of Protobuf types and every part of Settings scrolled with no bar at all; they now have one. Each part of Settings still keeps its own place.
- **Settings › Capture › Apps is right about HTTP/3** (Windows and Linux). With *Read chosen programs' HTTP/3* on, it said HTTP/3 is never caught, while tracewing was recording it. With it off, it now names what makes a program fall back to HTTP/2: tracewing refuses its HTTP/3, and passes the rest of UDP straight through. In all 15 languages.

## Updating

tracewing 0.1.0 finds this release on its own, at its daily look or at *Check now* in Settings › About:

- On Windows and macOS, *Download and install*, and it installs as tracewing quits.
- With the `.deb` or `.rpm`, About shows the commands to run, with the file's SHA-256.

**macOS:** 13 Ventura and newer. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized, so it opens without a detour.

**Linux:** the packages run on glibc 2.35 and newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12). They are not signed: `dnf` warns and goes on, and `zypper` needs `--no-gpg-checks`.

**Windows:** the installer is not signed, so SmartScreen asks the first time (More info › Run anyway). The installer and the `.zip` include Wintun 0.14.1 from wintun.net, which is what takes programs' connections with no proxy settings. Its own license is beside it, in `WINTUN-LICENSE.txt`.

`SHA256SUMS` holds every file's SHA-256.

tracewing is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later), and all of it is free: there is nothing to buy and nothing to sign in to. It also [builds from source](https://github.com/hivepaas/tracewing/blob/main/docs/building.md) on all three — one `make release`.
